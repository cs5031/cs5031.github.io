About the course

Analyze rigorously. Design strategically.

A graduate-level exploration of algorithmic techniques, proofs, complexity, and computational limitations.

⌁

Analyze complexity

Use formal complexity analysis, recurrences, randomized methods and amortized analysis.

◇

Design algorithms

Study network flow, string algorithms, LP, approximation and search strategies.

✓

Reason rigorously

Reason about NP-completeness, reductions, approximation guarantees and computability.
